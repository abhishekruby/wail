# Harsh Kiroween 2026 Judge Review: Wail

**Reviewed on:** Nov 20, 2026  
**Scope evaluated:** `README.md`, all files in `docs/planning/` (`00`–`10` + `wireframes.html`), all files in `.kiro/steering/`, official Kiroween 2026 rules (`https://kiroween.devpost.com/rules`), and Kiroween 2025 winners (`https://kiroween-2025.devpost.com/`).  
**Verdict on the plan as written:** **5.4 / 10 (weighted average across the 5 equal 20% criteria).** Disciplined engineering plan that will likely clear Stage One viability *if* built genuinely inside Kiro, but as written it will **not** place in the Top 3 or win Best Resurrection—it gets hit hard on Innovation (heavy prior-art collision with Nedagram and `ggwave`/Waver/Gibberlink, zero modern AI/DSP twist), Design (WCAG contrast failure in its own tokens + jarring horror/humanitarian clash + single-device judge dead-end), and Kiro execution gaps (missing Custom Agent/Power tasks, impossible Kiro Crew nightly schedule).

## 1. Scores Against the 5 Kiroween 2026 Judging Criteria

Official Stage Two rubric (`https://kiroween.devpost.com/rules`): 5 criteria, 20% (10 points) each.

### Potential Impact — **6 / 10**
The shutdown and disaster data establishes a real communication gap, and limiting scope to short, exact, checksum-verified text shows welcome realism about acoustic bandwidth. However, the headline pitch of "zero setup, no install from a store" contradicts `docs/planning/03-users-and-audience.md`'s own admission that Wail requires an online first visit before a crisis, leaving the same pre-blackout adoption barrier as Bridgefy while adding browser cache eviction and "best effort" iOS Safari fragility (including no `navigator.vibrate` on iOS). Worse, sending a 500-character notice takes ~40 seconds per cycle at `FAST`—two full minutes for a 3-cycle broadcast—and without sender authentication on unlocked broadcasts or real-room acoustic proof over noisy relief-camp speakers, a judge will doubt whether field teams would actually rely on this over megaphones, SMS, or Bluetooth mesh.

### Technological Implementation — **6 / 10**
On paper the Kiro plan hits strong notes—8 EARS specs, `fast-check` property tests that already caught a genuine `ggwave` silent-corruption bug, hooks, MCP, and a companion Kiro Crew app—showing far more engineering discipline than a typical hackathon prototype. In practice, however, every planning doc, `.kiro/steering/` file, and baseline test currently in the repo was authored outside Kiro before Kiro credits even arrived (`T0.1`), while `docs/planning/09-task-breakdown.md` omits any tasks for Kiro Custom Agents or Kiro Powers (both explicitly highlighted in the Kiroween 2026 rules) and references a non-existent `docs/setup/ci.yml` in `T0.3`. Most glaringly, `docs/planning/04-prd.md` commits to ≥ 5 nightly Kiro Crew torture runs before submission, yet `09-task-breakdown.md` schedules building `wail-nightwatch` on Nov 30–Dec 1 (leaving at most 2–3 nights before the Dec 3 internal deadline) and budgets only 2 hours to package it for Kiro Crew's App Kit.

### Design — **5 / 10**
The "1990s CRT modem terminal" concept fits Kiroween's Resurrection theme visually, and the four-screen flow is refreshingly sparse with explicit error states and calm-mode intent. That coherence breaks down under scrutiny: naming clinic patient-referral and cyclone-relief screens "Séance", "Crypt", and "Altar" creates a jarring tonal clash with low-literacy humanitarian personas, while `--phosphor-dim` (`#3f7f52`) on `--bg` (`#0b0f0c`) computes to **4.02:1**, failing the WCAG 2.1 AA `4.5:1` contrast requirement claimed in `docs/planning/07-ux-design-and-wireframes.md` and `.kiro/steering/haunted-ui.md`. Finally, `docs/planning/wireframes.html` omits a Listen-screen passphrase input, the IndexedDB `messages` store drops the ciphertext/salt/IV needed to unlock a message after receipt, the "spectrogram" is sketched as a flat 1D polyline rather than a time-frequency waterfall, and a judge evaluating alone on one laptop has no 1-click solo test on the home screen.

### Innovation — **4 / 10**
Judges in 2026 already recognize `ggwave` from Georgi Gerganov's `Waver` and the viral ElevenLabs hackathon winner `Gibberlink`, so wrapping `ggwave` 0.4.0 in a chunked Preact UI immediately reads as a thin product wrapper rather than a technical breakthrough. Worse, `docs/planning/02-research-market-competitors.md` severely understates **Nedagram** (`https://github.com/shayanb/Nedagram`), an existing open-source offline PWA built specifically for internet shutdowns that already ships compression, FEC, SHA-256 verification, ChaCha20-Poly1305 password encryption, and dual wideband/phone-call MFSK modes—directly colliding with Wail's core v1 feature list. Compared to Kiroween 2025 Resurrection winner **Faxi** (which paired obsolete fax hardware with an AI agent to turn handwritten faxes into live two-way emails and orders), Wail injects zero modern AI or novel DSP into the resurrected medium, burying its strongest original engineering (salted whitening + per-frame CRC-16 against `ggwave`'s silent-corruption bug) in an architecture doc instead of making it an unmistakable leap over prior art.

### Presentation — **6 / 10**
The 2:50 video script opens with the right hook—two phones visibly in airplane mode passing a message by sound—and committing to a published field-test table instead of hand-wavy claims will earn instant judge credibility. However, the `0:00–0:15` cold open will overrun 15 seconds unless it sends a tiny payload at `FASTEST`, the script never proves offline PWA cold-reload in airplane mode, and cramming four Kiro artifacts (spec, hook, shrinking PBT, Crew report) into 40 seconds risks turning the technical proof into unreadable tiny-font IDE blur. Equally dangerous, the plan assumes judges will pull out a second device to try the live URL, when most hackathon judges evaluate dozens of submissions solo on one laptop and will close the tab in 20 seconds if the home screen offers no instant single-device experience.

*(Benchmark context from Kiroween 2025 winners at `https://kiroween-2025.devpost.com/`: **InteractionKit** [Grand Prize] delivered a reusable developer toolkit with instant webcam wow; **UnitSet** [Frankenstein] fused canvas, AI, code, and live sandboxes; **SpookBnB** [Costume Contest] turned dark mode into an unforgettable flashlight escape room; **Faxi** [Resurrection] combined dead fax hardware with modern AI to solve a real human problem; **Necro-OS** [Resurrection track winner] delivered an immediately playable haunted Win95 AI takeover. Every standout winner had either a 5-second solo-playable wow or a genuine modern-AI twist on legacy tech. Wail's plan currently has neither.)*

## 2. The 5 Biggest Risks (Loss or Disqualification)

1. **Disqualification Risk — Insufficient / Retrofitted Kiro Usage (`kiroween.devpost.com/rules`):**  
   The official rules state explicitly that **insufficient use of Kiro results in disqualification at the judges' sole discretion**, and the 2026 rubric grades Technological Implementation almost entirely on depth and breadth of Kiro usage (IDE, CLI, Crew, specs, hooks, steering, MCP, Custom Agents, Powers). Right now, all 11 planning docs, all 6 `.kiro/steering/` files, `tests/ggwave-baseline.test.ts`, and `spikes/` were written outside Kiro while still waiting for Kiro credits (`T0.1`, credit request deadline Dec 1). If Kiro credits stall or `git log` shows heavy non-Kiro code generation with `.kiro/` bolted on late, Wail risks failing Stage One or tanking Technological Implementation.

2. **Fatal Innovation Loss — Direct Prior-Art Collision with Nedagram & `ggwave` "Wrapper" Label:**  
   Any judge who spends 30 seconds searching "send text over sound offline PWA" finds **Nedagram** (`github.com/shayanb/Nedagram`)—which ALREADY does offline PWA + MFSK + compression + FEC + SHA-256 + ChaCha20-Poly1305 encryption + phone-call mode for internet blackouts—plus **Waver**, **ChirpFile**, and **Gibberlink**. Because Wail delegates 100% of DSP/ECC to `ggwave` 0.4.0 and dismisses Nedagram in one inaccurate sentence, judges will score Innovation 3–4/10 unless Wail visibly differentiates on protocol engineering (broadcast carousel + salted whitening + codec-verified narrowband mode + signed broadcasts) and acknowledges prior art honestly.

3. **Impact & Viability Failure — Real-World Acoustics, Phone-Call Reality & the "Zero-Install" Contradiction:**  
   Lab white-noise tests (`docs/field-tests.md`) do not model room reverberation, cheap speaker clipping, crowd voices, or Android OEM audio HALs that ignore WebRTC `noiseSuppression: false`. Meanwhile, the offline codec simulation (`docs/research/codec-sim.md`) already proved Wail's planned default protocols (`AUDIBLE_FAST`/`AUDIBLE_NORMAL`) get **0/10 on AMR-NB calls** and `DT_*` gets **0/10 on AMR-WB 12.65k at `volume = 10`**. Compounding this, pitching "no prior install" while requiring online first-visit PWA caching—and listing `file://` Bluetooth HTML sharing as a stretch goal even though Chrome blocks `getUserMedia` outside Secure Contexts (`https://`/`localhost`)—will make skeptical judges question the entire real-world premise.

4. **Kiro Crew Bonus & Schedule Collapse (`wail-nightwatch` + Missing Artifacts):**  
   `04-prd.md` §3 sets a release target of **≥ 5 Kiro Crew nightly torture runs**, yet `09-task-breakdown.md` doesn't start `wail-nightwatch` until **Nov 30–Dec 1** (2–3 nights before Dec 3) and budgets only **2 hours** to package it for Kiro Crew's App Kit (`github.com/kirodotdev/KiroCrew`: agents/skills, MCP servers, cron jobs, dashboard UI). Add in missing `docs/setup/ci.yml` (`T0.3`), zero tasks for Kiro Custom Agents or Powers, and a noisy write-up hook (`T1.8`) that auto-appends spec edits into `docs/kiro-writeup.md`, and the Kiro story is set up to look rushed right before freeze.

5. **Design & Presentation Failure — Single-Device Judge Bounce + WCAG & Tonal Self-Goals:**  
   Judges almost always test live links solo on a laptop before watching or re-watching the video. Burying `Run self-test` on tab 4 (`Altar`) instead of putting a 1-click solo loopback / sample-decode experience on the home screen guarantees judges hear beeps into the void and move on. Simultaneously, `--phosphor-dim` (`#3f7f52` on `#0b0f0c` = **4.02:1**) fails WCAG AA on its own nav/status text, "Séance / Crypt / Altar" clashes with cyclone-relief and clinic-nurse personas, and storing no ciphertext/salt/IV in IndexedDB breaks post-receive passphrase unlocking.

## 4. Existing Data-Over-Sound Projects

| Project | Link | How Wail Differs (or Doesn't) |

| **ggwave / Waver** | [github.com/ggerganov/ggwave](https://github.com/ggerganov/ggwave) · [waver.ggerganov.com](https://waver.ggerganov.com) | Wail **uses `ggwave` 0.4.0 directly** as its modem; Wail adds multi-frame chunking (>140 B), salted whitening + CRC-16/32, broadcast carousel repeats, AES-GCM locking, crisis cards, and an offline PWA shell, whereas Waver is a raw single-frame demo. |
| **Nedagram** | [github.com/shayanb/Nedagram](https://github.com/shayanb/Nedagram) · [nedagram.com](https://nedagram.com) | **Closest direct competitor (major overlap):** already an open-source offline PWA for internet shutdowns with MFSK, compression, FEC, SHA-256 checks, ChaCha20-Poly1305 encryption, and phone-call mode; Wail differentiates only if it delivers multi-frame broadcast carousel, structured crisis cards, salted whitening, and published field reliability. |
| **ChirpFile** | [chirpfile.com](https://chirpfile.com) | Already wraps `ggwave` in a browser UI with local encryption for short text/file-key transfers; Wail adds offline PWA caching, multi-frame chunking, broadcast repeats, and crisis templates. |
| **Gibberlink** | [github.com/PennyroyalTea/gibberlink](https://github.com/PennyroyalTea/gibberlink) | Viral 2025 hackathon project using `ggwave` for AI-to-AI voice-call handoff—not a human crisis messenger, but it means judges already know `ggwave`'s sound and won't be amazed by raw FSK chirps alone. |
| **Quiet.js / libquiet** | [github.com/quiet/quiet-js](https://github.com/quiet/quiet-js) | Mature Emscripten/WASM browser acoustic modem (`liquid-dsp` + `libfec`, audible + 19 kHz ultrasonic + 40 kbps cable profiles); lower-level SDK rather than a crisis PWA, though technically more advanced at the DSP layer than `ggwave`. |

| **CopSonic** | Commercial ultrasonic DoS suite | Proprietary enterprise ultrasonic proximity/authentication platform; closed B2B pairing tech rather than an open offline broadcast messaging app. |
