# Product: Wail

Wail sends short messages between devices as sound, so people can share exact text when mobile data is cut. Hackathon: Kiroween 2026, category Resurrection (we revive the acoustic modem).

## Users

Relief coordinators, clinic staff and community organisers in shutdown- or disaster-hit areas (India and East Africa first). Secondary: judges testing on a laptop and a phone.

## What matters most, in order

1. Exactness: never show partial or altered text. A failed checksum shows nothing.
2. Zero setup: no account, no pairing, no server.
3. One-to-many: broadcast through a loudspeaker reaches a whole room.
4. Privacy when needed: optional lock with a spoken passphrase.
5. Clear feedback and accessibility: text and vibration for every sound event, calm mode.

## Scope

v1: text up to 500 characters; contact, location and notice cards; broadcast (3 repeats); lock; offline PWA; haunted modem UI.
Not in v1: images, video, accounts, servers, mesh relaying, ultrasound.
