# Product: Wail: Séance

Wail: Séance is a Halloween party game where a ghost speaks to every phone in the room through sound. No app, no Wi-Fi, no join code, no internet. Hackathon: Kiroween 2026, category Resurrection (we revive the 1990s dial-up modem as the voice of a ghost).

## Users

Party players, 4–8 at a table plus a host with a speaker. Secondary: judges on one laptop running the full game against 3 bots.

## What matters most, in order

1. Secret roles stay secret: each phone shows only its own role and clues, encrypted on a public broadcast.
2. Zero setup: open a link, no app, no Wi-Fi, no join code.
3. Fun in 10 seconds: the ghost speaks, roles arrive, the room erupts. A full game is 10–15 minutes.
4. Never show a wrong role: a failed checksum reveals nothing.
5. Clear feedback and accessibility: vibration for every event, calm mode (no flashing).

## Scope

v1: a complete game (Summon, roles, omens, banish, win check) with the acoustic modem engine, haunted UI, offline PWA, and play-alone solo demo for judges. Scope is R20–R27 in the PRD.
Not in v1: contact cards, location cards, accounts, servers, mesh relaying, ultrasound, vote by sound.

Full PRD and design: `docs/planning/04-prd.md` (v2.0, game requirements R20–R27 in EARS notation) and `docs/planning/11-seance-game-design.md`.
